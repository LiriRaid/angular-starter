# Code of Conduct

This document is a short, project-specific code of conduct inspired by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). It may be replaced by the full Contributor Covenant text before the first public release.

## Our standard

We want a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or opinion.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, focusing on the work rather than the person.
- Assume good faith and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces when someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile or use GitHub's built-in reporting tools. Reports are handled confidentially.
